# 2026 AMPO Planning Tools & Training Symposium: Resilience and Disaster Recovery (RDR) Tool Suite

The "Using the Resilience and Disaster Recovery Tool Suite to Support Resilient Transportation Infrastructure Project Priorization" session will take place on Wednesday, April 22, 2026 from 1 PM to 2:30 PM.

After an overview of the RDR Tool Suite at the beginning of the session, interested participants will have an opportunity to follow along with a series of interactive technical demos. These demos will showcase several of the key functionalities of the tool suite.

To install RDR and download the interactive demos before the workshop, please refer to the [Pre-Training Document](https://github.com/VolpeUSDOT/RDR-Public/blob/gh_pages/docs/AMPO_RDR_Pre-Training_Document.pdf). Workshop materials are available for download at [this link](https://public.huddle.com/b/vzaGBD/index.html).

If you have any questions during this setup process, please reach out to <RDR-Team@dot.gov>.
